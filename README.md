# Bergamot · Mind

A calm journal and notes app for shift workers — part of the citrus family (Citrus, Mandarin, Clementine, Calamansi).

- **Journal** — daily entries with mood and energy; the reflection methods (evening check-in, gratitude, Stoic review, weekly review, Start·Stop·Continue, wheel of life, pre-mortem, year in review, free write); prompts that know your shifts (after a night, before a run of nights, a day off); on this day; #tags, @people and +places; full-text search; gentle streaks; mood and energy against your shifts; dictation; export to Markdown or PDF; an optional per-device lock.
- **Notes** — fast capture, Markdown, pinned notes, folders and tags, checklists, `[[links]]` with backlinks, a note that becomes a task or links to a goal.
- **Tasks and habits** — due, priority, estimate, area, goal; habits with streaks that can tick themselves from your journal or another app; alarms through Google Calendar.
- **The day log** and a guided start.

Goals stay in Citrus Plan; Bergamot shows what moves them. Data lives in the browser and in your own Google Drive (`My Apps/hub/bergamot`).

Site: https://bergamot-mind.github.io/Bergamot/ · vanilla ES modules, no build step (`docs/`). Tests: `bash test/all.sh`.
